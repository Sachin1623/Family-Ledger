Privacy Policy for FamilyLedger
Last Updated: May 02, 2026
1. Introduction
FamilyLedger ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and disclose information when you use our mobile application (the "App").
2. Information We Collect
* Account Information: To use FamilyLedger, you must sign in via Google Authentication. We receive your name, email address, and profile picture from Google to create and manage your account.
* Financial Data: The App allows you to record transactions, categories, and family budget details. This data is stored securely in our database.
* Usage Information: We may collect basic technical data (e.g., device type, operating system version) to help us troubleshoot errors and improve the App's performance.
3. How We Use Your Information
* To provide and maintain the App's core functionality (ledger management).
* To synchronize your data across multiple devices.
* To authenticate your identity and secure your account.
* To provide customer support and respond to your requests.
4. Data Storage and Security
We use Google Firebase to store your data and manage authentication.
Storage: Your financial records are stored in a Firebase Firestore database hosted by Google.
Security: We utilize Firebase Security Rules to ensure that only you (and authorized family members if you share access) can read or write your personal data. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal information, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.
5. Third-Party Services
FamilyLedger uses the following third-party services:
* Google Play Services: Used for App functionality and updates.
* Firebase Authentication & Firestore: Used for user accounts and data persistence.
* Google Sign-In: Used for simplified account creation.
These services may collect information used to identify you. We encourage you to read the Google Privacy Policy.
6. Data Retention and Deletion
We retain your information for as long as your account is active.
Deletion: If you wish to delete your account and all associated data, you may contact us at sachin.rajputs@gmail.com. Upon request, we will remove your records from our servers within 30 days.
7. Children’s Privacy
FamilyLedger is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, we will delete it immediately.
8. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
