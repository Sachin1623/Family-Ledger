1. HOW TO DELETE YOUR ACCOUNT FROM WITHIN THE APP

To request deletion of your Family Ledger account and associated data using the app:

1. Open the Family Ledger app on your device.  
2. Sign in with the account you want to delete.  
3. Go to: Profile Delete Account.  
4. Read the on-screen warning about data deletion.  
5. Confirm the deletion request.

Once confirmed, your account will be marked for deletion and processed as described in the “Data we delete” section below.

---

2. HOW TO DELETE YOUR USER DATA:

You can do that by going to your expense line and click delete.
