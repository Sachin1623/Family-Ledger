## Target audience

Families and close friend groups who regularly share expenses — rent, groceries, trips, recurring household bills — and want one place to split and track them fairly. Secondary hook: the same group also plays games and chats together inside the app, so it's not just a "money app" they open reluctantly.

## Elevator pitch

FamilyLedger removes the awkwardness of tracking who-owes-who in a shared household or friend group — split bills instantly, never miss a recurring payment, stay on budget, and keep the whole group in the loop — while a built-in library of classic multiplayer games and group chat give everyone a reason to open the app together, not just when money's involved.

## Feature catalog (by category)

### 💰 Expense & Money Management (core)
- **Group expense splitting** — equally, by percentage, or exact custom amounts per person
- **Income tracking** — logged separately from expenses, net-spend visibility per group
- **Multiple groups** — separate ledgers for family, roommates, trips, friend circles, etc., each with its own currency
- **Recurring expenses** — auto-reminders and one-tap confirmation so rent/subscriptions/bills never get forgotten
- **Budgets & spend alerts** — set a budget per category/group, get notified as you approach it
- **Settlements** — one tap to see exactly who owes whom and settle up
- **Expense categories & payment methods** — organized, filterable spending history
- **Receipt attachments** — attach a photo to any expense
- **Group analytics** — visual breakdown of spending by category/member over time
- **Personal Loans ledger** — track informal loans to/from individuals outside of a group, with due-date reminders
- **Quick Add Expense** — fast-entry flow for logging a spend in seconds

### 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Group & Social
- **Group chat** — a dedicated conversation per group
- **Direct messages** — 1:1 chat between any two connected members
- **Activity feed** — a live, chronological feed of everything happening across your groups (expenses added, members joining, comments, reminders)
- **Invites** — invite by link, email, or straight to WhatsApp
- **Pokes/nudges** — a lightweight "hey, add today's expense" nudge to a group member
- **Weekly Recap** — a personal, auto-generated weekly + monthly summary (games played & with whom, expenses tracked, groups engaged, people chatted with), with a shareable branded image card — the exact material this request is for
- **Birthday reminders** — the app remembers group members' birthdays and prompts a shout-out

### 🎲 Games (built-in multiplayer game room — 7 titles)
All playable online with real-time turns, in-game chat, invites, and rematches:
- **27-Hand Rummy** — classic card game, full hand-by-hand scoring
- **Ludo** — 2–4 players, free color choice, team mode for 4 players, classic board rules
- **Business** — Indian Monopoly-style board game with property trading and auctions
- **Chess** — full rules-enforced 1v1
- **Sequence** — classic card-and-board sequence game
- **Sweep** — custom trick-taking card game
- **Scramble** — word-unscrambling game, single-player (fully offline) and multiplayer (up to 4, live race), with hints, shuffle, pass, word-meaning lookups, and a leaderboard

### ✅ Productivity
- **To-Do lists** — shared or personal task tracking with reminders
- **Shopping lists** — collaborative lists per group
- **Smart reminders** — spend reminders, inactivity nudges, recurring-bill due dates, budget thresholds — all customizable/toggleable
- **Financial calculators** — built-in quick calculators for common money math

### 🔒 Trust & Personalization
- **App Lock** — PIN or biometric lock on the app itself
- **Native push notifications** — real-time alerts for expenses, invites, chat, game turns, reminders
- **Feedback & support** — in-app feedback/bug-report system with direct admin replies
- **Offline-friendly** — core features (like single-player Scramble) work fully offline, syncing when back online
